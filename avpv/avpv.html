Who’s doing the doing ?

Active and passive voice tell the same story from different angles. Learn how to spot each one, then practice with a quick matching game and flash cards.

the doer → the receiver
The quick lesson

Same action. Different focus.

Find the subject, then ask: what is it doing?

Active voice

Doer → action → receiver

The subject performs the action. The sentence feels direct and easy to follow.

The chef baked the bread.

Passive voice

Receiver → action → by doer

The subject receives the action. The doer can come later—or be left out.

The bread was baked by the chef.

Quick clue: Passive voice often uses a form of be (is, was, were, been) + a past participle (made, carried, chosen). That clue helps, but always check who receives the action.
